# ISO 27001 Gap Assessment Checklist

A practical repository for structuring an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system (ISMS) gap assessment, evidence collection, risk review, and audit-readiness activities.

**Maintainer:** Nisha Chandra  
**Focus:** Cybersecurity governance, risk and compliance (GRC)

## Purpose

This checklist is intended as a working aid for security professionals, consultants, and organisations preparing for an ISMS gap assessment. It can help teams organise questions, evidence, ownership, and follow-up actions.

It is not a substitute for the licensed ISO/IEC 27001 standard, a formal audit, legal advice, or organisation-specific risk assessment.

## Suggested assessment workflow

1. Define scope, context, interested parties, and assessment criteria.
2. Identify applicable requirements and controls using an authorised copy of the relevant standard.
3. Gather evidence and record the source, date, control owner, and assessment notes.
4. Evaluate design and implementation separately; do not mark a control effective based on policy existence alone.
5. Record gaps, risk implications, accountable owners, corrective actions, and target dates.
6. Validate closure with evidence and document residual risk and limitations.

## Recommended evidence fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Requirement / control reference | Traceability to the applicable assessment criteria |
| Applicability and rationale | Explain why the requirement or control applies |
| Evidence reviewed | Identify records, interviews, configurations, or tests reviewed |
| Design assessment | Determine whether the control is appropriately designed |
| Implementation / operating evidence | Check whether it is implemented and functioning |
| Gap and risk | Describe the shortfall and its potential impact |
| Owner and action | Assign accountability and remediation |
| Validation date | Track follow-up and closure evidence |

## Research and improvement roadmap

Potential enhancements include:
- A clear assessment methodology and scoring rubric.
- Separate fields for control design, implementation, and operating effectiveness.
- Risk-based prioritisation with documented rationale.
- A change log and versioned assessment templates.
- Crosswalks to other frameworks only where mappings are verified and their limitations are explained.

## Important limitations

- Verify the current edition, amendments, and applicable requirements of the standard before use.
- Framework mappings are not automatically equivalent or interchangeable.
- Do not place confidential client, employer, or personal information in public issues or files.
- This repository is an educational and professional reference aid, not a certification or compliance guarantee.
