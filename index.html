About

A practitioner-led approach to cybersecurity research: understand the problem, evaluate evidence, map risks to controls, and communicate findings clearly.

Research principle
Good security research should be traceable to credible sources, transparent about its methodology, and clear about limitations. Practical recommendations should be testable and useful to the people responsible for managing risk.

Research approach

A repeatable process for building credible, useful research outputs.

01 · Define

Frame a narrow research question, identify the intended audience, and state the scope.

02 · Investigate

Review primary standards, peer-reviewed literature, official guidance, and reproducible evidence.

03 · Validate & publish

Explain the method, document findings and limitations, cite sources, and publish only authorised, non-confidential material.

Reference starting points

  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework — cybersecurity risk management.
  • OWASP — application security and testing resources.
  • NIST CSRC publications — technical and security publications.
  • Google Scholar — discovery of academic literature.

This site describes professional interests and ongoing portfolio work. It does not claim peer-reviewed publication or independent validation where those have not yet occurred. All technical testing should be performed only on systems owned by the researcher or explicitly authorised for testing.
